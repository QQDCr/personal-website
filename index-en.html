About Me

Hi, I’m Luo Jian. My work explores game mechanics, level design, and playable prototypes. I’m interested in how spaces, visual cues, and interactions come together to create a game experience.

In Light and Shadow, I explore light and darkness through puzzle mechanics, level sketches, and Blueprint implementation. This portfolio brings together the idea, the process, and what I learned from playtesting.
