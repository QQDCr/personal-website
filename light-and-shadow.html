01 / CONCEPT

In a world below ground,
let light lead the way.

The world

After ages of environmental destruction, the surface is reduced to sand and rock, forcing humanity underground. Light and darkness become constant companions as humans compete for territory with a physically weak but highly intelligent mimic species.

The player

The player is a pioneer exploring naturally formed underground caves to find new territory for their people. Light, shadow, and environmental mechanisms help reveal a path to the next level.

01

Follow the light

Follow lighting cues to identify interactive objects and discover where to go next.

02

Switch states

Switch between light and shadow. On-screen prompts in the tutorial introduce the controls for changing the character’s color.

03

Use reflections

Interact with mirrors to redirect light and work out a route toward the objective.

05 / PLAYTEST & REFLECTION

Finding the next steps through playtesting.

These notes summarize the project’s playtest feedback, preserving both the strengths identified and the issues still to address.

What worked

  • Guidance helps players understand their current objective.
  • The gameplay is easy to learn and does not require complex controls.
  • The gameplay progression offers variety and keeps the experience engaging.

Areas to improve

  • Some actions are restricted to specific colors; the relationship between character states and available actions needs refinement.
  • Uneven sizes and proportions across level areas affect the sense of space.
  • Small maps and limited opportunities for interaction weaken the exploration experience.
  • Overly strong, bright lighting undermines the atmosphere of an underground cave.
